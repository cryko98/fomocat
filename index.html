The Legend

Nine lives.

Every trader has one chart they still think about. The one they watched, hovered over, and closed.

That's why his eyes look like that — two enormous polished mirrors that reflect every chart on Solana at the same time. He just sits, paws neatly together, tail wrapped around himself, staring into the candles.

The teal glow in his ears isn't fur. It's the light of a screen that was never turned off.
